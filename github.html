How to publish your website on GitHub Pages

  1. Sign in at github.com, open the + menu at the top right and choose New repository.
  2. Type a repository name. For your personal site use USERNAME.github.io. Choose Public and click Create repository.
  3. Click Add file, then Upload files. Add your index.html and your other files, then click Commit changes.
  4. Open the repository's Settings tab, then Pages (under "Code and automation").
  5. Under "Build and deployment", set Source to Deploy from a branch, pick your main branch, and save.
  6. Wait. It can take up to 10 minutes. Then open https://USERNAME.github.io/. A normal repository is published at https://USERNAME.github.io/REPOSITORY/.

Sir E's tips

  • Your main page must be named exactly index.html.
  • Free accounts need a public repository for Pages. Make sure it is public.....
  • Never upload passwords, phone numbers or private details. Everything in a public repository can be seen.
  • Nothing changed? Refresh with Ctrl + F5 and wait a few minutes.
  • Menus can change. If something looks different, check GitHub's own Pages quickstart.
