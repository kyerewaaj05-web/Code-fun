How binary works in 60 seconds

Computers store everything as bits, tiny switches that are either 0 or 1. Eight bits make one byte, and one byte is enough for one basic character.

Each bit in a byte is worth double the one on its right:

1286432168421
01000001

Add up the places that hold a 1: 64 + 1 = 65. In ASCII and UTF-8, 65 is the letter A. So 01000001 means A, and 01001000 01101001 means Hi.

ASCII covers 128 basic characters: English letters, digits and punctuation. UTF-8 covers every language and emoji, using 1 to 4 bytes per character, and matches ASCII for the first 128.

Questions people ask

How do I convert text to binary?

Pick Text to binary, type your text, and the binary appears as you type. Each character becomes one or more 8-bit groups.

How do I decode binary to text?

Pick Binary to text and paste your 0s and 1s. Spaces and commas are ignored, but the total must be a multiple of 8.

Why must the length be a multiple of 8?

Every character is stored in whole bytes, and a byte has 8 bits. A missing bit means the last character is broken.

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. Everything runs inside your browser on your own device.

How do I turn binary into a decimal number?

Use the Number converter tab, or add up the place values that hold a 1, like 64 + 1 = 65.
