Privacy

We count visits. We do not track people.

Short, honest and in plain words.

The short version

  • No accounts and no passwords.
  • What you type stays in your browser. We never receive it.
  • Please never type phone numbers, emails or other private details anywhere on the site.

What is saved on your device

Only on your own phone or computer, never sent anywhere: your light or dark choice, Lite and Chaos mode, your Ok sir count, which cards you flipped, the card and nickname you chose, game scores, badges and difficulty, complaint tickets, dismissed notices, the daily message streak, your chat look, and whether you answered the biscuit banner.

The biscuit banner is a joke. There are no tracking cookies.

Visit counting and hosting

This site may count visits with a cookie-free counter. It counts page views, not people, and stores no personal details. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep basic visit records under its own policy. Links to other sites follow those sites' own rules.
